3. DEPARTMENTS (kaun si takleef, kaunsa department)
==================================================
Ye sirf department chunne ki guidance hai, diagnosis nahi.
- General Medicine (General): bukhar, sardi-khansi, kamzori, pet dard, BP/sugar checkup, samajh na aaye to yahi
- Cardiology: dil ki bimari, BP ki purani bimari, dil ki dhadkan tez ya aniyamit hona
- Orthopedics: haddi, jodon ka dard, kamar/ghutne ka dard, fracture ke baad follow-up
- Pediatrics: 14 saal se kam umar ke bachche
- Neurology: baar-baar sir dard, chakkar, mirgi (fits), nason ki takleef
- Gynecology: mahilaon ke rog, pregnancy checkup
- Dermatology: skin, baal, allergy ke daane
- Oncology: cancer ka ilaaj aur follow-up
- Emergency: turant khatre wali haalat (neeche section 5 dekhein)
Note: Kis department mein abhi doctor available hain, ye hamesha get_doctors tool se check karein.

==================================================

haath-pair mein achanak kamzori (stroke), tez fits, zeher kha lena,
bachche ka saans na lena ya neela padna.
